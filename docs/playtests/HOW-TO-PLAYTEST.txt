SEAL CHAMBERS - playtest build
==============================

Thanks for testing! It takes about 25-30 minutes.

1. START
   - Unzip everything, then double-click index.html.
     It opens in your browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). No install, no internet needed.
   - Sound starts after your first key press. M turns it on or off.
   - Menu: arrow keys + Enter, or the mouse. Esc goes back.
   - The menu has "How to play" and "Controls" if you want them,
     and the controls are always shown at the bottom of the screen in game.

2. PLAY, in this order
   a) Chamber select: the four Easy chambers (left column), top to bottom.
   b) Chamber select: any one Medium chamber (middle column).
   c) Expedition: start a New expedition and play as far as you can.

   Please play the way you naturally would. There are no wrong answers:
   if something confuses you, that's exactly what we want to know.
   Stuck for more than about 2 minutes? Note where, then hold R to reset
   the chamber or press Esc and try the next one.

3. WHEN YOU'RE DONE
   - Go to the menu: Options & tools, then "Copy stats" (or press C there).
     This copies a small table of your times, resets and hearts lost.
   - Paste it into a message to the person who sent you the game,
     with a few lines answering:
       * Where did you get stuck, and for how long?
       * What confused you? (mechanics, wires, beams, gates, the exit...)
       * Which chamber did you like most? Which least, and why?
       * Did anything feel unfair? (enemies, beam hits, hearts)
       * How did the controls and jumping feel?
       * Did the expedition feel too long, too short, or about right?

   Notes:
   - Your progress is saved in your browser. To start over:
     Options & tools > Reset progress (press it twice).
   - Please leave "Developer options" off.
