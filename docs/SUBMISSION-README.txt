SEAL CHAMBERS
=============
A 2D puzzle action-platformer prototype: 12 puzzle chambers built on four
interacting mechanics (Weight, Light, Phase, Gates), with combat, run
upgrades, seeded expeditions, Remix and Endless runs.

PLAY
- Offline: double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). It is the
  whole game in one file: no install, no server, no internet needed.
- Online:  https://0xahmedm.github.io/DU-Assignment/
- Sound starts after your first key press; M turns it on or off.
- The menu has "How to play" and "Controls"; the controls are also shown
  at the bottom of the screen in game.

CONTROLS
  Move ............ Left / Right  or  A / D
  Aim up .......... Up or W (up-attack, up-Bolt)
  Jump ............ Space or Z (hold = higher)
  Attack .......... X or J
  Crate ........... C or K (pick up / put down)
  Interact ........ E (take an upgrade on a pedestal)
  Throw / Bolt / Dash (abilities, once unlocked) ... V or L / F or I / Shift or O
  Reset chamber ... hold R
  Menu ............ Esc
